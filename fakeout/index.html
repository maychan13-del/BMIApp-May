Top 5 Fact-Check Tips · SIFT

  1. Stop. Pause before you share — emotion is a red flag.
  2. Investigate the source. Who wrote it? Are they real?
  3. Find better coverage. Do trusted outlets report the same?
  4. Trace to the original. Photos, quotes and stats travel without context.
  5. Ask a friend / teacher. Two brains beat one rumour. 💪
